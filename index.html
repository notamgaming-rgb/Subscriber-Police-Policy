01

Introduction

Subscriber Police (“the Bot”) helps Telegram group administrators require users to join a configured channel before proceeding through the group’s access flow. The Bot also provides a /start command and additional help and information commands.

This policy describes information that may be processed when you interact with these features.

02

Information That May Be Processed

To respond to Telegram updates and perform membership checks, the Bot may process information such as:

  • Telegram user IDs and chat or group IDs.
  • The configured channel’s identifier and membership-check results.
  • Commands, button interactions, and settings sent to the Bot.
  • Technical error records, if logging is enabled in the Bot or its hosting environment.

The Bot should not need your Telegram password or login verification code. Never share those credentials with anyone.

03

How Information Is Used

Information may be processed only as needed to operate and maintain the Bot, including to:

  • Check whether a user has joined the required Telegram channel.
  • Apply force-subscription settings configured by group administrators.
  • Handle bot commands and provide the relevant response.
  • Diagnose technical problems and help protect the service, where applicable.
04

/start Message & Commands

When a user sends /start, Subscriber Police can display an introductory message explaining that it helps manage required-channel subscriptions in Telegram groups.

The start message may also list these available commands:

  • /help — view help and basic instructions.
  • /tutorial — view setup or usage guidance.
  • /id — retrieve a Telegram ID where supported.
  • /donate — voluntarily support bot improvements, performance, and maintenance.

The Bot may process the command sent and related Telegram user or chat identifiers to provide the relevant response.

05

Storage & Retention

Whether data is stored and how long it is retained depends on the Bot’s implementation and hosting configuration. The operator should keep only what is needed to run the Bot, limit access to authorized people, and delete information when it is no longer needed, except where retention is legally required or reasonably necessary for security.

06

Sharing & Third-Party Services

The operator should not sell personal information. Telegram processes information to provide its platform and Bot API. A hosting or infrastructure provider may process technical data needed to run the Bot. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

07

Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information handled by the Bot. No online service can guarantee absolute security. The operator should keep the bot token, hosting credentials, and any database credentials private, and never place secrets in a public GitHub repository.

08

Questions & Deletion Requests

You may stop interacting with the Bot at any time. If you want to ask about information that may be stored or request deletion, contact the Bot owner or administrator through their official Telegram contact. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

09

Telegram & External Policies

The Bot relies on Telegram for Telegram-related functionality. Telegram independently handles information under its own policies. You can read Telegram’s Privacy Policy.

10

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised when the Bot’s features or information-handling practices change. The date shown at the top should be updated whenever a material change is made.
